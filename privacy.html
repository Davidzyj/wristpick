Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Overview

WristPick is developed and maintained by Zhou Yajie. This policy covers the watchOS app and distinguishes it from this support website.

No app data collection

The app works offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or track your personal data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases, third-party SDKs, or network services.

Data on your watch

Your number rules, saved rules, language and haptic preferences, and the latest 50 draw records are stored in the app’s local storage. They are not sent to the developer. System-managed backups or transfers may include this data according to your Apple device settings; the developer does not control those backups.

Deleting your data

In History, swipe left to delete a record, or choose Clear history to remove every draw. In Presets, swipe left on a saved rule to delete it. Settings → Reset all data deletes the app’s local history, saved rules, and preferences. This does not delete copies in system-managed backups. There is no account to delete.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for that purpose, and do not use it for advertising. Do not send passwords or sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add cookies, analytics, external fonts, or tracking scripts. GitHub may process IP addresses and standard request logs to provide hosting and security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your email provider may also process messages under its own terms.

Children and changes

The app does not request personal information from children or other users. If support correspondence contains information you wish removed, contact us. Material policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
