Technical Support

Effective October 29, 2026

Generate numbers

Tap Pick a number. Tap the range summary to set the minimum, maximum, and quantity. Values range from −999 to 9999, with up to 12 numbers per draw.

Adjust the range

Open Minimum or Maximum. Rotate the Digital Crown or tap − / +, then tap Done. If the bounds would cross, the opposite bound is adjusted. Quantity is reduced automatically if the range is too small for a draw without repeats.

Repeats and sorting

In Draw rules, Allow repeats permits repeated values in a draw. Turn it off to draw unique values. Sort results displays the generated values in ascending order; it does not change which values are drawn.

Save a favorite

In Draw rules, optionally enter a name and choose Save rule. Presets contains three quick-start rules and up to 12 saved rules. Duplicate rules cannot be saved twice.

Find earlier results

History stores your latest 50 draws. Open a record and choose Use this rule to reuse its settings. Swipe left to delete a record, or Clear history to delete all draw records.

Change language or haptics

Open the gear icon → Language and choose System, 简体中文, English, or 日本語. Haptic feedback can also be disabled in Settings. The app follows the watch language by default.

Independent and offline

Requires Apple Watch with watchOS 10.0 or later. No companion iPhone app, account, or internet connection is required for app functions. Apple Watch setup and App Store installation follow Apple’s device requirements.

What does randomness mean?

Numbers are generated using the operating system’s random number generator. Each draw is independent. Results are for everyday choices; they do not predict future events and are not intended for security credentials, regulated prize draws, or certified auditing.
